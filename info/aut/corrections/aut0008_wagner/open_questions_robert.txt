    Ammoniated Sulfate Particles, Bertozzi 2024
    (this has two joint first authors: Bertozzi and Wagner)"

   If joint first authorship should be visible, it needs a new role and a
   place to record it. Worth asking Ottmar whether it matters for the
   statistics he wants.
